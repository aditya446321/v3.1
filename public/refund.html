Refund & Cancellation Policy

Because products are digital, purchases are generally final after successful delivery or access. Please review the package and price carefully before payment.

Before payment

Check the package name, included features, price, and any active offer before completing payment.

After payment

Once a digital product or access has been successfully delivered, cancellation or refund may not be available except where required by applicable law or where the service has failed to provide the purchased product.

Incorrect or duplicate payment

If you believe an incorrect amount was charged or a payment was duplicated, contact support with your Order ID and transaction/reference information so the issue can be reviewed.

Consumer rights

Nothing in this policy is intended to remove rights that cannot lawfully be excluded under applicable consumer-protection law.
