Privacy Policy

This policy explains how information submitted through the store may be used to operate and support orders.

Information collected

Checkout may collect your name, contact information, selected package, order amount, and optional payment reference/UTR.

How information is used

  • To create and manage orders.
  • To provide customer support.
  • To review payment references and prevent misuse.
  • To maintain operational, accounting, security, or legal records where appropriate.

Payment credentials

The store does not request or store UPI PINs, banking passwords, card PINs, or OTPs.

Data retention

Records may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary for service, security, accounting, dispute, or legal purposes.

Contact

Use the configured support contact shown on the store for privacy questions.
